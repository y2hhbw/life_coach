# Anti-Vision

> Inspired by: [How to fix your entire life in 1 day](https://x.com/thedankoe/status/2010751592346030461) by @thedankoe

> [!danger] Core Warning
> If you choose to escape and be inefficient today, this will be your reality in 2029.

### 1. Shattered Mornings
The alarm went off three times before you got up, feeling groggy. The first thing you did wasn't thinking, but picking up your phone to scroll through 30 minutes of meaningless news, and watching 2 hours of brainless short dramas. You feel a familiar, faint self-loathing.

### 2. Stagnant Assets
Opening [[nav_tracker.md]], the net asset value curve has been flat or falling for months, and your savings are dwindling; survival might even become a problem. You are not executing logic, but trading "by feel", or holding onto losing positions because you are afraid to cut losses. Your dream of financial freedom is slipping away like sand in an hourglass, and you can't do anything about it.

### 3. The Illusion of "Efficiency"
You spend the whole day tweaking your Obsidian theme or organizing notes you will never look at again. You comfort yourself by calling it "infrastructure work", but deep down you know: you are just avoiding the hardest trading model, or the most core code module.

### 4. A Failing Body
Due to a lack of exercise, chaotic routines, and poor diet, you feel exhausted. Your smoking addiction acts up again, and you smoke while looking at the dull-eyed, pot-bellied version of yourself in the mirror. You are no longer the "person with a vision", but a shell manipulated by habits.

### 5. The Ultimate Price
On a Tuesday in 2029, you sit in the exact same spot, facing the exact same failure. You realize you had the chance to change everything, but because of every "I'll do it tomorrow" back then, you have finally become the person you detest the most.
