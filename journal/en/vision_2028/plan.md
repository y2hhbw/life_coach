# vision 2028 - 3-Year Vision

> From 2026 to 2028, these three years will define the next stage of my life. For every decision ask: Does this bring me closer to the ideal version of myself in 2028?

---

## 2028 North Star

**Core Vision:** By the end of 2028, I will have achieved success in my chosen professional field, attaining financial freedom, physical and mental well-being, and having deep relationships. My working style is controlled by me, my time is invested in truly important things, and I can arrange my life freely according to my own wishes.

**Verification Criteria:**
- **Career Development:** Established a stable source of income in core business, with a stable customer/user base, realizing a sustainable profit model.
- **Financial Status:** Income fully covers living expenses, asset size reaches the expected target, achieving financial freedom, not working for money on things I don't want to do.
- **Personal Influence:** Established a certain level of influence in the professional field, building a personal brand through content creation and social media.
- **Health Status:** Completely improved bad living habits, established a healthy lifestyle, physical fitness indicators and body condition are better than the average level of peers, full of energy.
- **Interpersonal Relationships:** Have relationships capable of deep communication, harmonious relationships with family and friends.
- **Lifestyle:** Good work-life balance, have time to pursue personal interests, continuous improvement in the quality of life.
- **Continuous Growth:** Maintain a state of learning and growth, constantly expanding the boundaries of my abilities.

---

## Critical Aspects

<!-- Non-negotiable core principles -->

1. **Physical and Mental Health** - Without health, everything else loses meaning. Adequate sleep and a healthy diet every day are non-negotiable; maintain a good physical state.
2. **Values** - Pursue long-termism, no shortsightedness, do not take risks for short-term gains; stick to my beliefs, maintain continuous optimization and improvement; no matter how small, persisting will eventually produce a qualitative change.
3. **Financial Freedom** - Financial freedom is an important goal, consider whether it helps achieve this goal when doing anything.

---

## 3-Year Roadmap (2026-2028)

### 2026: Building the Foundation
**Theme: Year of Deep Plowing and Breakthrough**
- Deepen efforts in the core business area, establish stable workflows and income models.
- Launch multiple projects, validate business models, establish an initial customer base.
- Continuously expand business boundaries, try new opportunities and areas.
- Establish a sustainable work and life system, ensuring continuous improvement in physical and health conditions.
- Start building a personal brand and social media influence.

### 2027: Accelerated Growth
**Theme: Year of Accelerated Development**
- Building on 2026, further optimize business models and raise income levels.
- Expand project scales, increase the number of projects with stable profitability.
- Continue to expand business boundaries, find new growth points.
- Continuous growth of personal brand and influence, establishing industry recognition.

### 2028: Breakthrough and Harvest
**Theme: Year of Financial Freedom and Transition**
- Achieve financial freedom goals, income and assets reach the expected level.
- Mature and stable business system, owning multiple profitable projects.
- Personal influence reaches new heights, becoming an influential person in the field.
- Prepare for the next three years (2029-2031).

---

## Decision Filters

Every time facing a choice ask yourself:

1. **Does this bring me closer to my ideal self in 2028?**
2. **Does this align with my Critical Aspects?**
3. **Looking back in three years, will I be proud of making this decision?**

If the answer is no, bravely say no.
